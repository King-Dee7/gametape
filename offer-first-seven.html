FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will people I know see my videos?+

Not unless you want them to. Your first three videos stay private: saved to drafts, or posted with "Only you" selected. By the time you post publicly, you've already done it three times.

Will I make money in the 7 days?+

No, and we won't pretend you will. TikTok Shop opens to creators at 1,000 followers. The First Seven gets you started: you'll know how it works, have seven videos made, and have a plan for getting to 1,000.

What if I have 0 followers right now?+

That's exactly who it's for. You don't need an audience to start.

Do I need a camera, lights or a mic?+

No. Just the phone you already have and things already in your house. One lesson shows you the whole setup in about a minute.

How much time does each day take?+

About 15 minutes: a 4-minute video from Rickey and an 11-minute task. Small enough to do before work or on your lunch break.

Is it only for the US?+

Yes. TikTok Shop runs in a few countries, but this course is built for the US TikTok Shop, for people 18 or older in the US.

What happens after Day 7?+

You keep posting on your plan. If you want the next four weeks with Rickey and a small group, that's Shop Ready, and you'll hear about it. It's optional. The First Seven is complete on its own.
