In Your Corner

Rickey Eugene Brown is a Los Angeles-born actor, filmmaker, and content creator known for his dynamic on-screen presence, comedic storytelling, and ability to connect with audiences across entertainment and digital media. His acting credits include the blockbuster hit Central Intelligence, starring Kevin Hart and Dwayne “The Rock” Johnson, HBO’s Winning Time: The Rise of the Lakers Dynasty, Your Honor, and work on the NFL Madden video game franchise.

Rickey’s comedy skits have gone viral numerous times across social media platforms, amassing over 1 billion views across platforms. His unique blend of humor, relatable characters, and creative storytelling has helped him build a massive digital audience and establish himself as a versatile entertainer and sought-after content creator. His commercial acting work includes campaigns for McDonald’s, Subway, and Best Buy, while his social media brand collaborations include Meta, Puma, Gap, Von Dutch, Casio, DJI, Hover, and many more.

Whether he’s bringing characters to life on screen, creating viral comedy, collaborating with major brands, or developing original film projects, Rickey brings a distinct creative perspective and a passion for entertaining audiences.
